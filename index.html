About

approach & background

I'm a junior studying Data Science at Montana State University in Bozeman, graduating May 2027. My foundation is in R and Python — statistical modeling, and machine learning concepts like Bayesian scoring, clustering, PCA, and association rules — but what I actually enjoy is turning that into something with a real interface that real people use.

Right now that means working as a pool technician at Umvelt Bathhouse here in Bozeman, where handling water chemistry and daily facility operations turned into building the shift-management software the place now runs on — full-stack, from database to deploy, on my own initiative.

Outside of school and work, climbing is the throughline. Most of what I build starts the same way a route does: read the actual constraint, find the smallest line through it, and go.
